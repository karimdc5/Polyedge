How PolyEdge Works
Everything you need to know to trade with narrative intelligence.
◈ YES & NO — THE BASICS
✅ YES
You believe the event WILL happen. Buy YES tokens. If correct, you profit the difference from $1.
❌ NO
You believe the event WON'T happen. Buy NO tokens. If correct, you profit the difference from $1.
Example: "Will Bitcoin hit $150K?" trades at 37¢ YES. Buy $100 of YES → if Bitcoin hits $150K you get $270 back ($170 profit). If not, you lose $100.
◈ NARRATIVE LAG SCORE (0–100)
Measures the gap between where the narrative is and where the price is.

HIGH (65+): Crowd is behind. Price moved but media/public hasn't caught up. Potential edge.
MEDIUM (40–64): Some lag present. Worth watching.
LOW (<40): Market is efficiently priced. Less edge available.
◈ HOW LAG IS CALCULATED
Price uncertainty (close to 50/50) + thin liquidity (easy to misprice) + volume velocity (money moving fast). All three signals combine into the Lag Score.
◈ EXPECTED VALUE (EV)
EV = (your probability × profit) − (1 − your probability × stake). Positive EV = mathematically good bet over time. Negative EV = avoid.
